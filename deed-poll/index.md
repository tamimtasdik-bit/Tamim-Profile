<h3>
<span>What is a Deed Poll?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>A <strong>Deed Poll</strong> is a legally binding document that acts as evidence of a change of name. It allows you to update your name on official documents such as your passport, driving licence, bank accounts, and more. This document confirms that you:</p><ol><li>Are abandoning your previous name.</li><li>Are adopting your new name.</li><li>Will use your new name at all times going forward.</li></ol>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>At Lawfinity Solicitors, we assist individuals and families with all aspects of the Deed Poll process, including:</p><ol><li><strong>Drafting the Deed Poll</strong><br/>We ensure your Deed Poll is professionally prepared, meeting all legal requirements for acceptance by relevant authorities.</li><li><strong>Certification of the Deed Poll</strong><br/>We provide certified copies of your Deed Poll, ensuring it is accepted by organisations requiring proof of your name change.</li><li><strong>Guidance on Updating Official Records</strong><br/>Changing your name involves updating various records and documents. We offer guidance on notifying authorities, institutions, and service providers, such as the Passport Office, HMRC, and banks.</li><li><strong>Name Changes for Children</strong><br/>If you’re applying for a name change on behalf of a child, our experts will guide you through the additional legal requirements, such as obtaining parental consent.</li></ol>

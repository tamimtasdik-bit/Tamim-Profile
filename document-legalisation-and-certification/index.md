<h3>
<span>What is Document Legalisation?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p><strong>Legalisation</strong> is the process of verifying the authenticity of a document for it to be recognised by foreign authorities. It involves certifying the document’s validity, often through an apostille or consular authentication, depending on the destination country’s legal requirements. At Lawfinity, we handle every aspect of the legalisation process, ensuring compliance with international standards.</p>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>
<h3>
<span>What is Document Certification?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p><strong>Certification</strong> confirms that a document is a true and accurate copy of the original or that its contents are verified. This service is often required for purposes such as visa applications, international transactions, academic qualifications, or corporate operations.</p>
</div>

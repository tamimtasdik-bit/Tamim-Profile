<h3>
What is a Marriage Visitor Visa? </h3>









<p>A <strong>Marriage Visitor Visa</strong> allows foreign nationals to enter the UK with the sole purpose of getting married or registering a civil partnership. This visa permits you to stay in the UK for up to six months. It is ideal for couples where one partner is a UK resident or citizen, and the other is visiting for the specific purpose of marriage.</p>

 












<h3>
Who Can Apply for a Marriage Visitor Visa? </h3>









<p>To be eligible for the <strong>Marriage Visitor Visa</strong>, you must meet the following key criteria:</p><ul><li><strong>You must be 18 years or older</strong> at the time of application.</li><li>You <strong>intend to marry or register a civil partnership</strong> within six months of entering the UK.</li><li><strong>You must be in a genuine relationship</strong> and able to prove this to UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI).</li><li>You <strong>must have sufficient funds</strong> to support yourself during your stay and prove you will leave the UK at the end of your visa.</li><li>You <strong>cannot switch to a spouse visa</strong> or extend the Marriage Visitor Visa from within the UK; this visa is strictly for short stays.</li></ul>

 












<h3>
What Documents Do You Need for Your Marriage Visitor Visa Application? </h3>









<p>To ensure a successful application, you must submit the correct documents. These include:</p><ul><li><strong>Proof of your relationship</strong>: This may include photos, letters, and other supporting documentation showing a genuine relationship with your partner.</li><li><strong>Evidence of the marriage or civil partnership plans</strong>: A confirmed appointment at a UK registry office or marriage venue can strengthen your application.</li><li><strong>Financial evidence</strong>: This includes bank statements, payslips, or evidence of income to prove you can financially support yourself while in the UK without public funds.</li><li><strong>Travel documentation</strong>: Your flight bookings and accommodation details.</li></ul><p>You’ll also need to prove that you plan to return to your home country once your visa expires.</p>

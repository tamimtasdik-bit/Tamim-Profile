<p>We are pleased to provide you with a comprehensive fee scale for the services offered by Lawfinity Solicitors. As a firm committed to delivering exceptional professional services, we strive to ensure clarity and transparency in our fee structure.</p><h6>Fee Schedule:</h6><p>At Lawfinity Solicitors, we pride ourselves on delivering high-quality services and, as such, we do charge fees for our services. Institutions such as the Law Centre and Citizen’s Advice Bureaus provide free immigration advisory services.</p><p>Lawfinity Solicitors operates on a fixed fee basis.

<h3>
<span>What is a Power of Attorney?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>A <strong>Power of Attorney</strong> is a legal document that allows you to appoint another person (known as your attorney) to make decisions or manage affairs on your behalf. This document can be tailored to provide broad or limited powers, depending on your needs.</p><ol><li><strong>General Power of Attorney</strong><ul><li>Grants broad authority to your appointed attorney to handle various matters, such as financial transactions, property management, or other personal affairs.</li><li>Typically used when you are temporarily unavailable, such as travelling abroad, or when you need assistance due to illness or other personal circumstances.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Specific Power of Attorney</strong><ul><li>Restricts the attorney’s authority to a particular task or matter, such as selling a property, managing a bank account, or representing you in legal or business transactions.</li><li>Ideal for one-off transactions where clear and limited authority is required.</li></ul></li></ol>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h6>
<span>When Might You Need a Power of Attorney?</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<div>
<div>
<ul><li><strong>Travelling Abroad</strong>: Delegating authority to manage your finances or property while you are away.</li><li><strong>Managing Property</strong>: Authorising someone to buy, sell, or manage real estate on your behalf.</li><li><strong>Health or Illness Concerns</strong>: Ensuring someone you trust can act in your best interests if you’re unable to do so.</li><li><strong>Business Transactions</strong>: Appointing someone to handle contracts, banking, or other professional dealings.</li><li><strong>Legal Proceedings</strong>: Granting authority for legal representation in a specific case.</li></ul> </div>

<h3>
<span>What is a Travel Authorisation?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>A <strong>travel authorisation document</strong> is a formal letter or affidavit that grants permission from one or both parents or legal guardians for a child to travel with another party. This document is often required by airlines, border authorities, or consulates to prevent misunderstandings or legal complications during international travel.</p>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>
<h3>
<span>When Do You Need a Travel Authorisation?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>A travel authorisation is typically required in situations such as:</p><ul><li>A <strong>child travelling with one parent</strong> and needing the consent of the other parent.</li><li>A <strong>child travelling with grandparents, relatives, or a trusted family friend</strong>, requiring written permission from both parents or legal guardians.</li><li>A <strong>child travelling for school trips, sports events, or holidays</strong> where parental consent is mandatory.</li><li>When visiting countries that enforce strict travel policies for minors.</li></ul>
</div>
</div> </div>

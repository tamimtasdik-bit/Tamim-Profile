<span>
                                Child Under 18 as a British Citizen            </span>
</div>

</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p><span>The process of </span><b>registering a child under 18 as a British citizen</b><span> allows individuals who are not automatically British citizens by birth to apply for British nationality. Children who are under 18 can apply for citizenship through registration, provided they meet certain requirements. The </span><b>UK Nationality Act 1981</b><span> governs this process, offering a route for children born outside the UK or children of non-British citizens to acquire British citizenship.</span></p>

<div>
<h3>
<span>Eligibility Criteria for Child Registration</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p><span>There are several scenarios in which a child under 18 may be eligible for registration as a British citizen:</span></p><ol><li><b> Children Born in the UK to Parents with Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)</b></li></ol><p><span>If a child is born in the UK to parents who are settled (i.e., they hold ILR or permanent residence), the child may automatically become a British citizen.</span></p><ol><li><b> Children Born Outside the UK to British Parents</b></li></ol><p><span>Children born abroad can register as British citizens if one of their parents is a British citizen or settled in the UK. The parent must have either been a British citizen at the time of the child’s birth or obtained ILR before the child’s registration application is made.</span></p><ol><li><b> Children Who Have Lived in the UK for a Period of Time</b></li></ol><p><span>If a child has lived in the UK for a certain period, they may be eligible for registration as a British citizen, even if they are born outside the UK. These children need to have lived in the UK for a minimum of </span><b>5 years</b><span>, with no more than 90 days of absence in the last year of residence.</span></p><ol><li><b> Children Whose Parents are British Nationals by Descent</b></li></ol><p><span>Children born abroad to parents who are British citizens by descent (i.e., a parent who was born outside the UK but became a British citizen) may also apply for registration as a British citizen. This is possible if they meet certain requirements, such as living in the UK for at least 3 years.</span></p><ol><li><b> Children Born in the UK to Parents Who Are Not British Citizens</b></li></ol><p><span>A child born in the UK may apply for registration if their parents were not British citizens or settled in the UK at the time of the child’s birth, but the parents have since obtained British citizenship or settled status.</span></p><ol><li><b> Children Who Have Been Adopted by British Citizens</b></li></ol><p><span>If a child is adopted by British citizens, they may be eligible to apply for British citizenship through the registration process, provided the adoption meets the required criteria.</span></p>
</div>
</div> </div>

</div>

<div>
<h3>
<span>Key Requirements for Registration</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p><span>To register a child as a British citizen, you will typically need to provide the following:</span></p><ul><li><ul><li><b>Proof of the child’s identity</b><span> (e.g., passport or birth certificate).</span></li></ul></li></ul><ul><li><b>Evidence of the child’s residency</b><span> in the UK (if applicable).</span></li></ul><ul><li><ul><li><b>Parent(s)’ immigration status</b><span> (e.g., proof of ILR, British citizenship).</span></li></ul></li></ul><ul><li><b>Evidence of the child’s good character</b><span>, if the child is aged 10 or older.</span></li></ul><p><b></b><b>Application form</b><span> and the appropriate registration fee.</span></p>
</div>

<div>
<h6>
<span>Application Process for Child Registration</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<div>
<div>
<p><span>The process for registering a child as a British citizen involves completing an </span><b>application form</b><span> (Form MN1 for children under 18) and submitting it to the </span><b>Home Office</b><span>. The application will be assessed based on the child’s eligibility and the parent’s or guardian’s immigration status.</span></p><ol><li><b> Filling out the Form</b></li></ol><p><span>Complete the required form, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the child and their parents. You will need to provide supporting documents, including proof of the child’s identity and residency.</span></p><ol><li><b> Submitting the Form</b></li></ol><p><span>After completing the form and gathering the necessary documents, you will submit the application to the Home Office along with the required fee.</span></p><ol><li><b> Home Office Decision</b></li></ol><p><span>Once submitted, the Home Office will process the application. Processing times may vary depending on the specific circumstances of the application. The Home Office will notify the applicant of their decision.</span></p> </div>

<div>
<h6>
<span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<ol><li><b> Can a Child Apply for British Citizenship if They Are Born Outside the UK?</b></li></ol><p><span>Yes, a child born outside the UK can apply for British citizenship through the registration process if they meet certain eligibility criteria, such as having a British parent or living in the UK for a qualifying period.</span></p><ol><li><b> What Happens If the Application Is Refused?</b></li></ol><p><span>If an application for registration is refused, you may have the right to appeal the decision. It is advisable to seek legal advice if your application is rejected, as you may still be able to pursue other avenues for citizenship.</span></p><ol><li><b> Can I Apply for British Citizenship for My Child If They Are Over 18?</b></li></ol><p><span>Once a child turns 18, they are no longer eligible for registration as a British citizen. However, if they meet the eligibility criteria, they may still apply for British citizenship through the naturalisation process.</span></p><ol><li><b> How Long Does the Registration Process Take?</b></li></ol><p><span>The processing time for registering a child as a British citizen can vary. On average, it takes around </span><b>6 months</b><span> for the Home Office to process applications, but this can change depending on the case.</span></p><ol><li><b> Is There a Fee for Registering a Child as a British Citizen?</b></li></ol><p><span>Yes, there is a fee for registering a child as a British citizen. The fee amount is reviewed annually, and it is important to check the current fee before submitting your application.</span></p> </div>

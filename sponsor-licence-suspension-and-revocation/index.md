<h3>
<span>Understanding Sponsor Licence Suspension and Revocation</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>The Home Office holds strict guidelines for Sponsor Licence compliance, and failing to adhere to these requirements can result in a suspension or, in more serious cases, a permanent revocation of your licence. A suspended or revoked licence has profound effects, including the restriction or removal of your rights to sponsor overseas talent, affecting recruitment, operations, and reputation. Lawfinity Solicitors is here to offer expert guidance and advocacy at each stage, with services that include:</p><ol><li>Suspension Review and Representation: If your licence has been suspended, we conduct a comprehensive review of your practices and prepare strategic responses to address the Home Office’s concerns effectively.<br/><br/></li><li>Pre-Action and Judicial Reviews: In cases where a revocation appears imminent or unjust, we support clients in initiating pre-action and judicial reviews to challenge the Home Office’s decision when viable.<br/><br/></li><li>Compliance Remediation: We guide businesses in implementing corrective measures to address specific compliance breaches, helping to restore trust with UKVI and secure your licence status.</li></ol>

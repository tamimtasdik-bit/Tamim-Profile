<h5><span>Introduction:</span></h5>

</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>Lawfinity Solicitors, a provider of high-quality legal advice and client care services, is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards in our legal practice. We value your feedback and encourage you to communicate with us in the event of any concerns or dissatisfaction with our services. This Complaint Handling Policy outlines our commitment to addressing and resolving complaints in a timely and professional manner.</p>

<div>
<h5>SRA Principal:</h5>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>Principle 5 of the SRA Code of Conduct requires individuals regulated by SRA Regulation (CRL) to inform clients fully and honestly about their complaint’s procedures. This includes the client’s right to refer a complaint to the Legal Ombudsman or CRL where appropriate.</p>

<div>
<h6>Complaints Procedure:</h6>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<h6>2.1 Initial Acknowledgment:</h6><p>Upon receiving your complaint, Lawfinity Solicitors will promptly acknowledge it within three working days. Along with the acknowledgement, we will provide you with a copy of this Complaints Handling Procedure.</p><h6>2.2 Record Keeping:</h6><p>Your complaint will be recorded in our central register, and a dedicated file will be opened to investigate and address your concerns.</p><h6>2.3 Investigation and Response:</h6><p>We will conduct a thorough investigation into the issues raised in your complaint. Within 15 working days of sending the acknowledgement letter, we will send you a detailed written response outlining our findings and providing suggestions for resolving the matter.</p><h6>2.4 Meeting Option:</h6><p>Alternatively, if deemed appropriate, we may invite you to a meeting to discuss the issues you’ve raised. In such cases, we will follow up with a written communication within three working days of the meeting, summarising any agreed-upon solutions.</p><h6>2.5 Resolution Measures:</h6><p>In suitable cases, we may offer solutions, which could include an apology, a reduction in your bill, or a refund of any payment received.</p><h6>2.6 Timelines:</h6><p>Lawfinity Solicitors aims to resolve all complaints within six weeks of receiving them. If we anticipate that the resolution may take longer, we will inform you promptly and provide an estimated timeframe.</p>

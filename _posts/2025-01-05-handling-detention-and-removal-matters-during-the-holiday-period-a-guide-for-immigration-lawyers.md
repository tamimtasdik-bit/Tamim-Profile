<p>The holiday season is a time when most people look forward to rest and relaxation. However, for immigration lawyers, this period often comes with heightened challenges, especially when dealing with urgent detention or removal cases. Detention centres do not close, removal flights continue to operate, and clients face the same risks as during any other time of the year.

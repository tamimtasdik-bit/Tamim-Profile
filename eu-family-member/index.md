<h3>
<span>EU Family Member or Dependent Visa: Join Your Loved Ones in the UK</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>If you are a family member or dependent of an <strong>EU national</strong>, you may be eligible to join your loved one in the UK. The UK’s <strong>EU Settlement Scheme</strong> allows family members and dependents of EU citizens to apply for <strong>pre-settled status</strong> or <strong>settled status</strong> based on their relationship with the EU national. If you’re considering moving to the UK to live with an EU family member, understanding the process and eligibility for a <strong>family member or dependent visa</strong> is crucial.</p>

</div>

Our expert immigration lawyers will provide tailored advice and ensure your application is submitted successfully, giving you the best chance of reuniting with your family in the UK.</p>

<h3>
<span>What is the EU Family Member or Dependent Visa?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>The <strong>EU family member or dependent visa</strong> enables the close family members of EU nationals (who have <strong>settled</strong> or <strong>pre-settled status</strong> under the EU Settlement Scheme) to join them in the UK. These family members may include:</p><ul><li><strong>Spouses or civil partners</strong></li><li><strong>Unmarried partners</strong> (in a durable relationship)</li><li><strong>Children under 21</strong> (biological or adopted)</li><li><strong>Dependent parents or grandparents</strong></li></ul><p>This visa ensures that family reunification can happen smoothly, allowing families to stay together in the UK, regardless of their nationality.</p>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div>
<h6>
<span>Eligibility Requirements for EU Family Member or Dependent Visa</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<div>
<div>
<p>To be eligible for this visa, the following conditions must be met:</p><ol><li><strong>Relationship to the EU National</strong>: You must be a family member (spouse, child, parent, etc.) or dependent of an EU national who is living in the UK and has <strong>pre-settled</strong> or <strong>settled status</strong>.</li><li><strong>Proof of Relationship</strong>: You must provide proof of your relationship with the EU national. This could include marriage certificates, civil partnership certificates, or proof of cohabitation and dependency.</li><li><strong>Living Together</strong>: For some relationships, particularly for <strong>unmarried partners</strong>, you may need to demonstrate that you have been living together for at least two years.</li><li><strong>Residence Requirement</strong>: The EU national must be either living in the UK or planning to move to the UK, and they should be able to demonstrate that they have <strong>pre-settled</strong> or <strong>settled status</strong> under the EU Settlement Scheme.</li></ol> </div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div>
<h6>
<span>How to Apply for the EU Family Member or Dependent Visa</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<div>
<div>
<p>Applying for the EU Family Member or Dependent Visa typically involves the following steps:</p><p><strong>Step 1: Check Eligibility</strong></p><p>Ensure that you meet the eligibility requirements for the <strong>family member</strong> or <strong>dependent visa</strong>. Our team at <strong>Lawfinity Solicitors</strong> can assist with verifying your eligibility.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Gather Documents</strong></p><p>You’ll need to provide:</p><ul><li><strong>Proof of Identity</strong>: Passport or national ID card.</li><li><strong>Proof of Relationship</strong>: Marriage certificate, birth certificate, or other official documentation to prove your family link.</li><li><strong>Proof of Residence</strong>: Evidence that the EU national has <strong>pre-settled</strong> or <strong>settled status</strong>.</li></ul><p><strong>Step 3: Submit Online Application</strong></p><p>Submit your application online through the UK government’s official immigration website. If you need assistance with the online form, we are here to help ensure everything is correctly filled out.</p><p><strong>Step 4: Attend Biometrics Appointment</strong></p><p>You may need to attend a biometrics appointment where your fingerprints and photograph will be taken for identification purposes.</p><p><strong>Step 5: Await a Decision</strong></p><p>Once your application is submitted, the UK Home Office will process it. Processing times may vary depending on individual circumstances.</p> </div>

<div>
<h6>
<span>Benefits of EU Family Member or Dependent Visa</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>The <strong>EU Family Member or Dependent Visa</strong> provides several benefits for you and your family:</p><ul><li><ul><li><strong>Live in the UK</strong>: You’ll have the right to live in the UK and stay with your loved ones.</li><li><strong>Work and Study</strong>: You are eligible to work and study in the UK while holding this visa.</li><li><strong>Access Public Services</strong>: You can access services such as the NHS and other public benefits.</li><li><strong>Path to Settled Status</strong>: After residing in the UK for five years, you may be eligible to apply for <strong>settled status</strong>under the <strong>EU Settlement Scheme</strong>, giving you permanent residency.</li></ul></li></ul>

<h6>
<span>FAQs About the Graduate Visa</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p><strong>1. an Unmarried Partner Apply for an EU Family Member Visa?</strong></p><p>Yes, unmarried partners can apply for an <strong>EU Family Member Visa</strong> if they can prove they have been in a <strong>durable relationship</strong> for at least two years. The Home Office will assess your relationship and living arrangements.<br/><br/><strong>2. Can My Child Apply for a Family Member Visa?</strong></p><p>Yes, children under 21 can apply for a family member visa if they are a direct dependent of an EU national with <strong>settled</strong> or <strong>pre-settled status</strong>.<br/><br/><strong>3. How Long Does It Take to Process the Application?</strong></p><p>Processing times can vary, but typically, it takes around <strong>8 weeks</strong> for a decision on the application. Delays may occur if additional information or documentation is required.<br/><strong>4. Can I Work with an EU Family Member Visa?</strong></p><p>Yes, you can work and study in the UK while on the <strong>EU Family Member Visa</strong>.<br/><br/><strong>5. What If I Want to Extend My Visa?</strong></p><p>If you want to extend your visa or transition to <strong>settled status</strong> after 5 years, we can assist you in applying for <strong>settled status</strong> once you become eligible.</p>

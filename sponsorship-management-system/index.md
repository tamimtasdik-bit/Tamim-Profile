<h3>
Sponsorship Management System (SMS) Maintenance </h3>









<p>At Lawfinity Solicitors, we understand the pivotal role the Sponsorship Management System (SMS) plays in managing and maintaining compliance for businesses holding a Sponsor Licence. The SMS is a vital tool for handling the administrative responsibilities of sponsorship, from reporting migrant activities to updating business details as per UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) requirements. Lawfinity Solicitors offers comprehensive SMS Maintenance services, ensuring your organisation remains compliant and safeguarded against Home Office scrutiny.</p>

 







<p>Our specialist team provides businesses with structured support, guidance, and expertise in managing their SMS account effectively, offering peace of mind when it comes to Sponsor Licence obligations.</p>

 










 

 












<h3>
Why SMS Maintenance is Essential for Sponsor Licence Holders </h3>









<p>The Sponsorship Management System is not just a reporting tool—it’s the foundation for demonstrating ongoing compliance with Home Office regulations. A lapse in SMS maintenance can lead to penalties, compliance failures, or even revocation of your Sponsor Licence. Lawfinity Solicitors offers tailored SMS Maintenance services, ensuring your business:</p><ol><li>Adheres to Reporting Duties: We help your organisation fulfil its duty to report changes in migrant employees’ status, including any changes in job role, absences, and terminations.</li><li>Stays Updated with Compliance Requirements: By keeping your business details current in the SMS, Lawfinity helps you avoid compliance breaches that can arise from inaccurate records.</li><li>Maintains Control over Access: Our services include advising on SMS access levels and user roles, ensuring only authorised personnel have access, which enhances security and accountability.</li></ol><p>With Lawfinity’s assistance, managing the SMS becomes a seamless process, letting you focus on your business’s growth while remaining compliant with UKVI standards.</p>

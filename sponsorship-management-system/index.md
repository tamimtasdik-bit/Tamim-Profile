## Corporate immigration support

Businesses that sponsor or plan to sponsor overseas workers need immigration processes that are legally compliant and workable in practice. We provide focused advice on the issue, identify any compliance risks and help the business prepare the relevant application, response or internal process.

## A tailored approach

The right strategy depends on the organisation's sponsor history, workforce needs, records and the specific Home Office issue involved. We therefore begin with the facts and documents before recommending the next step.

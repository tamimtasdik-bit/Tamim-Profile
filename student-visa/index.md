<h4>
<span>Key Requirements for a UK Student Visa</span> </h4>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p><strong>1. Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)<br/></strong>To apply for a UK Student Visa, you need a valid <strong>CAS</strong> from a UK university or educational institution that is a licensed student sponsor. The CAS is a unique reference number issued by your university, detailing your course and duration.<br/><strong><br/>2.Financial Requirements<br/></strong>You must demonstrate that you have sufficient funds to support yourself during your studies, which includes tuition fees and living expenses.</p><ul><li><strong>Tuition Fees</strong>: You need to show proof of sufficient funds to cover your first year of tuition.</li><li><strong>Living Costs</strong>:</li><li><strong>£1,334 per month for up to 9 months if studying in <strong>London</strong>.</strong></li><li><strong><strong>£1,023 per month for up to 9 months if studying <strong>outside London</strong>.<br/></strong></strong><ul>Funds must be held for at least 28 consecutive days in a recognised bank account and must not be older than 31 days from the date of your visa application.</ul></li></ul><p><span><b>3. English Language Requirement<br/></b></span>You need to prove your proficiency in English by passing an approved English language test such as <strong>IELTS for UKVI</strong><span> or by meeting the language criteria outlined by your university. Certain exemptions apply if you are from an English-speaking country or have completed a degree taught in English.</span></p><p><strong>4. Tuberculosis (TB) Test</strong><br/>Students from certain countries must undergo a <strong>Tuberculosis test</strong> at a clinic approved by the UK Home Office. This is a mandatory requirement if you are applying to stay in the UK for more than six months.</p><p><strong>5. Dependants (Updated for 2024)<br/></strong>Under the new immigration rules effective from <strong>1 January 2024</strong>, only certain students can bring family members to the UK:</p><ul><li><strong>Eligible to bring dependants</strong>:<ul><li>Students enrolled in <strong>postgraduate research programmes</strong> (e.g., PhD or other RQF Level 8 courses).</li><li><strong>Government-sponsored students</strong> pursuing courses longer than 6 months.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Not eligible</strong>:<br/>Students enrolled in <strong>taught master</strong><strong>’</strong><strong>s degrees</strong> or lower-level courses are no longer permitted to bring dependants, except under specific exemptions.<br/>A dependant is typically a:</li><li>Spouse or civil partner</li><li>Unmarried partner (in a relationship for at least two years)</li><li>Child under 18 years oldIf you qualify to bring dependants, you must also demonstrate additional funds:</li><li>£845 per month per dependant (for up to 9 months) if studying in <strong>London</strong>.</li><li>£680 per month per dependant (for up to 9 months) if studying <strong>outside London</strong>.</li></ul>
</div>

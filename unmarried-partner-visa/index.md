About UK Unmarried Visa            

<h3>
UK Unmarried Partner Visa </h3>









<p>Are you in a long-term, committed relationship with a British citizen or someone settled in the UK? The UK Unmarried Partner Visa is an ideal route for couples who want to live together in the UK without formalising their relationship through marriage or civil partnership. This visa allows you to live, work, and build a life in the UK for up to 33 months, with the option to extend and eventually apply for permanent settlement.</p>

<h3>
Who is Eligible for the UK Unmarried Partner Visa? </h3>









<p>The <strong>Unmarried Partner Visa</strong> is for individuals who are in a <strong>genuine and ongoing relationship</strong> with a British citizen or someone settled in the UK. The key eligibility criteria include:</p><ul><li><strong>Relationship Duration</strong>: You and your partner must have been living together for at least <strong>2 years</strong>.</li><li><strong>Partner’s Status</strong>: Your partner must be a British citizen, settled in the UK, or have <strong>Indefinite Leave to Remain</strong>(ILR).</li><li><strong>Cohabitation</strong>: You must live together in a relationship akin to marriage.</li><li><strong>Financial and Accommodation Requirements</strong>: You must meet specific income and accommodation criteria to show that you can support yourselves in the UK.</li></ul><p>If you meet these criteria, you may be eligible for the <strong>Unmarried Partner Visa</strong>.</p>

 












<h3>
Financial and Accommodation Requirements for the UK Unmarried Partner Visa </h3>









<p>To qualify for the <strong>Unmarried Partner Visa</strong>, you must demonstrate that you and your partner meet the <strong>minimum financial requirement</strong>, which is:</p><ul><li><strong>£29,000 per year</strong> income or savings.</li><li>Alternatively, if you do not meet the income threshold, savings or other forms of financial support may be considered.</li></ul><p>You must also provide evidence of <strong>suitable accommodation</strong> where both you and your partner can live together in the UK.</p>

 

















<h6>
Step-by-Step Guide to Applying for the UK Unmarried Partner Visa </h6>









<p>Applying for a UK Unmarried Partner Visa involves several steps:</p><ol><li><strong>Complete the Online Application</strong>: Start by filling out the visa application form online. This will differ based on whether you are applying from inside or outside the UK.</li><li><strong>Pay the Application Fee and Healthcare Surcharge</strong>: The visa application fee for in-country applicants is £1,048, and £1,538 for those applying outside the UK. Additionally, the <strong>Healthcare Surcharge</strong> of £1,035 per person per year applies.</li><li><strong>Biometric Appointment</strong>: You will need to attend a biometric appointment for fingerprinting and photographs.</li><li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Submit essential documents, including proof of your relationship, financial evidence, and your partner’s UK status.</li></ol>

<h6>
What to Expect After You Apply </h6>





<p>Once your application is submitted, you will typically receive a decision within <strong>8-12 weeks</strong>. If approved, you will be granted a visa to live in the UK for <strong>up to 33 months</strong>. After this period, you may apply for an extension, allowing you to stay in the UK for another 30 months. After <strong>5 years</strong> on the Unmarried Partner Visa, you can apply for <strong>Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)</strong>.</p>

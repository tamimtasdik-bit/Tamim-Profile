<h3>
<span>Graduate Visa: Your Pathway to Post-Study Work in the UK</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>The UK Graduate Visa, often referred to as the <strong>Post-Study Work Visa</strong>, provides international students the opportunity to stay and work in the UK after completing their studies. This visa allows graduates to gain valuable work experience, enhancing their career prospects and opening pathways to potential long-term residency.</p>
</div>
</div> </div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>At <strong>Lawfinity Solicitors</strong>, we assist graduates in navigating the application process, ensuring a seamless transition from student life to the professional world in the UK.</p>

<h3>
<span>What Is the Graduate Visa?</span> </h3>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<p>The Graduate Visa allows international students who have successfully completed an eligible course in the UK to remain in the country for:</p><ul><li><strong>Up to 2 years</strong> (for undergraduate or master’s degree holders).</li><li><strong>Up to 3 years</strong> (for PhD or other doctoral graduates).<br/>This visa is a fantastic opportunity to gain work experience, seek skilled employment, or even explore entrepreneurship.<br/><br/><b>Eligibility Requirements</b><p>To qualify for a Graduate Visa, you must meet the following criteria:<br/><br/><span>1. </span><span><b>Successful Completion of an Eligible Course<br/></b></span>You must have completed a degree or other eligible qualification from a <strong>UK higher education provider</strong><span> with a track record of compliance. Eligible courses include:</span></p></li><li>Bachelor’s degree</li><li>Master’s degree</li><li>PhD or doctoral qualifications</li><li>Certain professional courses (e.g., law or medicine)<br/><span><br/>2. </span><span><b>Valid Student Visa<br/></b></span>You must have a valid <strong>Student Visa</strong><span> (or Tier 4 visa) at the time of your application.<br/></span><span><br/>3. </span><span><b>Course Completion Notification<br/></b></span>Your university must have informed the Home Office that you have successfully completed your course.<br/><span><br/>4. </span><span><b>No Maintenance Funds Requirement<br/></b></span>Unlike other visa categories, the Graduate Visa does not require you to show proof of funds for maintenance.<br/><span><br/>5. </span><span><b>English Language Proficiency<br/></b></span>Since you completed your studies in the UK, you will have already met the English language requirement.</li></ul>
</div>

<div>
<h6>
<span>Can You Bring Dependents on a Graduate Visa?</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<div>
<div>
<p>You can bring dependants if they were already in the UK as your dependants on a Student Visa. New dependants cannot be added under the Graduate Visa unless exceptions apply for children born in the UK during your Student Visa period.</p><p><strong>Benefits of the Graduate Visa</strong></p><ul><li><strong>Work Flexibility</strong>: No restrictions on the type of work you can undertake.</li><li><strong>No Sponsorship Requirement</strong>: You do not need a job offer or sponsorship to apply.</li><li><strong>Entrepreneurship Opportunities</strong>: You can start your own business while on this visa.</li><li><strong>Pathway to Skilled Worker Visa</strong>: After gaining work experience, you may be eligible to switch to a Skilled Worker Visa.</li></ul> </div>

<div>
<h6>
<span>FAQs About the Graduate Visa</span> </h6>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<div>
<div>
<div>

<div>
<ol><li><strong>Can I extend my Graduate Visa?</strong><br/>No, the Graduate Visa cannot be extended. However, you may switch to other visa categories, such as the Skilled Worker Visa, if eligible.</li><li><strong> Can I study on a Graduate Visa?</strong><br/>Yes, you can study, but it cannot be a course sponsored by a student sponsor.</li><li><strong> What happens if my Graduate Visa application is refused?</strong><br/>You may have the right to appeal or request an administrative review. Our solicitors can assist you in challenging a refusal.</li></ol>
